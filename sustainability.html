Built differently

Sustainability isn't an add-on.
It's a design principle.

Most podcasts don't think about their environmental footprint. We do — from the London spaces we record in, to the partners we work with, to the CO₂ our listeners generate by streaming us. Not performance. Proof.

Our commitments

Three things we're actively building around

Not future promises — design constraints we're working within from day one.

01

Build sustainability in visibly

A tree planting scheme tied to subscriber milestones. CO₂ transparency built into the website. Sustainability-aligned partners only. Competitors aren't doing this — that's the opportunity.

In progress
02

Design for circularity

Knowledge doesn't have to be one-directional. Listener voices shape future content. Repeat guests build on earlier conversations. Spaces and materials: cafes, libraries, museums across London, chosen with intention.

Active
03

Grow with purpose, not just speed

Local UK partners. Sustainability-aligned brands. The right 1,000 founders matters more than 10,000 wrong ones. Our growth strategy reflects that: audiences that matter more than numbers that look good.
